# Distribution quick card
**Version 1.1.0** · Food bank shift tracker · 2026-11-11 · Print one per distribution station

---

## AI READING INSTRUCTION

Read `[SPEC]` blocks for the steps. This card is a summary of the distribution guide.

---

## Each Family

**[SPEC]**
1. Front desk calls back a card: check the number and details, then **Confirm card**.
2. Enter a weight for each cart shown: **Cart 1 (lb)**, **Cart 2 (lb)**, **Cart 3 (lb)**. One cart per TEFAP bag.
3. Families of 5–7, 9–11 or 13+: add extras, tick **Extras added**, and note **What was added** if you like.
4. **Mark distributed**.
5. Details wrong? **Correct details**, change them, **Save correction**.
6. Read any **Note from the front desk** on the card.

## During the shift

**[SPEC]**
- Items returned: enter the weight under **Returned items**, **Add returned weight**.

## End of shift

**[SPEC]**
1. Under **End of shift: Door Dash and Helping Hands**, for each partner Family: tap the partner, enter **Card #**, **# in Family**, Senior Box, extras and cart weights, then **Add partner Family**.
2. Check **Totals**.
3. **Close shift**. Any card not distributed is listed: finish it, record **Left without food**, or ask staff to delete it.
4. **Yes, close the shift**.

## If something goes wrong

**[SPEC]**
- **A cart has no weight** or **Extras added is not ticked**: tap **Go back** and fix it. Tap **Mark distributed anyway** only if the food truly was not weighed or no extras were given.
- **Shift is closed** but needs a fix: ask staff to reopen it.
- **Connection lost**: keep going. Changes wait on the device and send themselves later.
- More help: tap **Help** at the top of the screen.
