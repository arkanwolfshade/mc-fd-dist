# Distribution quick card
**Version 1.0.0** · Food bank shift tracker · 2026-11-08 · Print one per distribution station

---

## AI READING INSTRUCTION

Read `[SPEC]` blocks for the steps. This card is a summary of the distribution guide.

---

## Each family

**[SPEC]**
1. Family arrives: **Confirm**.
2. Enter **Load 1**, **Load 2**, **Load 3** as needed.
3. **Mark distributed**.
4. Details wrong? **Correct details**, change them, **Save correction**.
5. Family left before being served? **Left without food**, then confirm.

## During the shift

**[SPEC]**
- Items returned: enter the weight under **Returned items**, **Add returned batch**.

## End of shift

**[SPEC]**
1. Door Dash and Helping Hands: tap the partner, enter families and weight, **Add last cards**.
2. Check **Totals**.
3. **Close shift**. Any card not distributed is listed: finish it, record **Left without food**, or ask staff to delete it.
4. **Yes, close the shift**.

## If something goes wrong

**[SPEC]**
- **No weight entered**: go back and enter the weight, unless the food truly was not weighed.
- **Shift is closed** but needs a fix: ask staff to reopen it.
- **Connection lost**: keep going. Changes wait on the device and send themselves later.
- More help: tap **Help** at the top of the screen.
