# Distribution quick card
**Version 1.1.0** · Food bank shift tracker · 2026-11-11 · Print one per distribution station

---

## AI READING INSTRUCTION

Read `[SPEC]` blocks for the steps. This card is a summary of the distribution guide.

---

## Each Family

**[SPEC]**
1. Front desk calls back a card: check the number, details and any **Note from the front desk**, then **Confirm card**.
2. Weigh each cart shown (one per TEFAP bag): **Cart 1 (lb)** and so on.
3. Families of 5–7, 9–11 or 13+: add extras, tick **Extras added**, note **What was added**.
4. **Mark distributed**. Details wrong? **Correct details** first.

## During the shift

**[SPEC]**
- Items returned: enter the weight under **Returned items**, **Add returned weight**.

## End of shift

**[SPEC]**
1. **End of shift: Door Dash and Helping Hands**: for each partner Family, tap the partner, fill in the card, then **Add partner Family**.
2. Check **Totals**.
3. **Close shift**. Any card not distributed is listed: finish it, record **Left without food**, or ask staff to delete it.
4. **Yes, close the shift**.

## If something goes wrong

**[SPEC]**
- **A cart has no weight** or **Extras added is not ticked**: **Go back** and fix it, or **Mark distributed anyway** if it truly can't be.
- **Shift is closed** but needs a fix: ask staff to reopen it.
- **Connection lost**: keep going. Changes wait on the device and send themselves later.
- More help: tap **Help** at the top of the screen.
